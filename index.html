CHAPTER 01

BEFORE THE NAME

The person before the brand.

Before Sagacious Tehilla, before TAS, before the books and the speaking and the Mystics — there was just a boy. Curious. Quiet in ways that surprised people. Loud in ways that surprised him.

He describes himself as socially awkward, weird, irritating, bored, talk active. He doesn't live like the stereotypical marketer. He doesn't care much about movies, football, or music.

He reads. He talks. He collaborates. He loves intelligent arguments. He studies people. He loves God. He believes in prayer. He hates tribalism.

This is who he was before the world gave him a title.
